Derogatory Marks — What Credit Karma Is Showing You
Grouped label, not a separate FICO factor
What it is
Credit Karma groups all serious negative marks under "Derogatory Marks" — it is their consumer-friendly label, not a separate FICO category.
Where it hits
Almost all derogatory marks damage Payment History (35%) — the missed payments, defaults, charge-offs. That is where most of the score drop lives.
Loans vs cards
Installment loans (car, student, personal) do not affect utilization the way credit cards do. FICO calculates revolving utilization separately. Loan balances matter much less to your Amounts Owed score than card balances.
Double hit
Marks like charge-offs can hit twice — once under Payment History (stopped paying) and once under Amounts Owed (balance still showing). That is why they drop scores harder than a simple late payment.

Today7 Years10 Years
270+ days late
Federal student loan enters default after 270 days of non-payment. Score drops 50–150 pts. Government can garnish wages and tax refunds without a court order.
Rehabilitation
Make 9 on-time payments in 10 months — the default is removed from your credit report entirely. This is unique to federal student loans. No other derogatory mark can be erased this way.
Year 2–5
Without rehabilitation, impact fades as positive history layers on top — but the late payment marks that led to default remain separately.
Year 7
Removed from report completely ✓ (or sooner via rehabilitation)
💡 Student loan rehabilitation is the only derogatory mark that can be legally removed before 7 years without disputing an error. If you have a defaulted federal loan, contact your loan servicer immediately and start the 9-payment rehabilitation program. Recovery after removal: +50–150 pts.
1
Timing
Pay before your closing date, not your due date
The bureau takes a snapshot on your statement closing date. Pay 3–5 days before it closes and the bureau sees almost nothing. Your score reflects low utilization immediately next cycle.
⬆ +20–50 pts within one billing cycle
2
Utilization
Ask for a credit limit increase every 6 months
If your limit goes from $5,000 to $10,000 and your spending stays the same, your utilization just got cut in half overnight. Most issuers grant this automatically with good payment history.
⬆ +10–40 pts depending on current utilization
3
History
Never close your oldest card — ever
That card is the anchor of your credit age. Even if you never use it, keep it open with a small recurring charge on autopay. Its age is silently boosting your score every single month.
⬆ Prevents a –15 to –40 pt drop from closing it
4
Utilization
Become an authorized user on an old card
If someone you trust has a 10-year-old card with low utilization and perfect history, being added as an authorized user puts that entire history on your report. You don't even need to use the card.
⬆ +20–80 pts depending on card's age and history
5
Inquiries
Pre-qualify before you apply anywhere
Almost every major issuer offers a pre-qualification tool using a soft pull — zero impact on your score. Only do the real application once you know you're likely approved. Rejections still leave hard inquiries.
⬆ Saves 5–10 pts per avoided hard inquiry
6
Disputes
Pull your free report and dispute every error
1 in 5 credit reports has an error. Pull yours free at annualcreditreport.com and look for wrong balances, accounts you don't recognize, or late payments marked incorrectly. Winning a dispute can jump your score fast.
⬆ +20–100 pts if an error is successfully disputed
7
Payments
Pay twice a month if you spend heavily
High spenders can look like high utilizers even if they pay in full. Solution: make a mid-cycle payment before closing date, then pay the rest on due date. Low balance reported, zero interest paid.
⬆ +15–40 pts by lowering reported utilization
8
Protection
Freeze your credit when you're not applying
A credit freeze blocks anyone from opening new accounts in your name — free at all three bureaus. Doesn't affect your score at all. Unfreeze only when you need to apply. Best protection against identity theft.
⬆ Prevents –50 to –150 pts from fraudulent accounts
9
Recovery
Call your issuer after a first-time late payment
If you've had a spotless history and miss a payment once, call the issuer and ask for a goodwill removal. Many will remove it from your report if it's your first offense. It works more often than people think.
⬆ +50–100 pts if the late payment is removed
10
Momentum
Monitor monthly — what you track, you improve
Use a free tool like Credit Karma, Experian, or your bank's built-in monitor. Watching your score monthly keeps you aware of what's moving it. Errors and fraud get caught early. You can't fix what you don't see.
⬆ Ongoing — catch issues before they cost 50+ pts
